Vision & Mission
Our Vision

To build a society where cooking oil is used safely, responsibly, and sustainably, while preventing environmental damage caused by improper disposal and transforming used cooking oil into a valuable resource that supports public health, clean energy, and a circular economy.

Our Mission

To raise public awareness of the health and environmental risks of reused and improperly disposed cooking oil, while providing accessible collection systems for safe and sustainable disposal. Through partnerships with recycling and energy industries, the organization transforms used cooking oil into renewable energy and economic value, supports community participation and waste-to-income initiatives, and contributes to Indonesia’s long-term environmental and climate sustainability goals.
