About Me
Student

Hi! My name is Ajmarel Mizarelly Ajazih, and I’m currently a 10th grader at Sekolah Bogor Raya, pursuing the International Baccalaureate Diploma Programme. My subject combination includes English A HL, Business Management HL, Global Politics HL, Math AA SL, Indonesian B SL, and Environmental Systems and Societies SL. This curriculum not only challenges me academically but also supports my broad interests in global affairs, leadership, and the arts.

I have a deep passion for music—I play the piano and enjoy singing. Outside of the arts, I’m also heavily involved in public speaking and critical thinking through debating and Model United Nations. These activities have allowed me to develop my voice, refine my arguments, and connect with peers who share similar ambitions.

My commitment to service and leadership extends beyond the classroom. I’m actively involved in various initiatives including student council, nonprofit organizations, and MUN committees. Whether it's helping organize events or contributing to community projects, I strive to make a meaningful impact wherever I can.

Balancing academics, extracurriculars, and leadership roles can be challenging, but I find it incredibly rewarding. Each experience helps shape me into a more thoughtful, capable, and socially responsible individual. I'm excited to keep growing and making a difference—one initiative at a time.
