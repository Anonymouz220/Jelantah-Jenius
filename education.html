Environmental Impact

Did you know that just one liter of used cooking oil can contaminate up to one million liters of clean water? Our educational mission is to empower communities with the knowledge to prevent this environmental catastrophe.

Health Risks

Reusing cooking oil multiple times creates harmful compounds that can lead to serious health issues. We teach families how to identify when oil is no longer safe for consumption.
